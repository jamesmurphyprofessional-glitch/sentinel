Sentinel — free version
=======================

A simple helper for Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 14 or later).
It explains which Mac settings to change and who is on your Wi-Fi.

Install
1. Double-click “Install Sentinel”
2. If macOS blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway

You do not need Xcode.

Uninstall
Quit Sentinel, then drag /Applications/Sentinel.app to the Trash.
