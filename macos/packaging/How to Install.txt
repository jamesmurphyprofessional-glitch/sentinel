How to install Sentinel
=======================

You do not need Xcode, Terminal, or any extra software.

1. Double-click “Sentinal Distro.zip” if it is still a zip file.
   A folder will appear.

2. Open that folder.

3. Double-click “Install Sentinel”.
   (If your Mac asks whether to open it, choose Open.)

4. Sentinel is copied into Applications and opens by itself.

If your Mac says Sentinel cannot be opened
------------------------------------------
That is normal for apps not from the App Store.

1. Open System Settings
2. Click Privacy & Security
3. Scroll down
4. Click Open Anyway next to Sentinel

How to use it
-------------
1. Press “Check this Mac”
2. Tick the box that this is your network
3. Press “Look at my Wi-Fi”

To uninstall later
------------------
Open the Applications folder, drag Sentinel to the Trash.
